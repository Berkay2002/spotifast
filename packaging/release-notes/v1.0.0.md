Spotifast 1.0.0 is the first release of this app as its own project. It brings in crmne's Spotifast up to [0.13.3](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.13.3), with this app's own features on top, and from now on it numbers its own versions. The built-in updater keeps following this repository's releases.

## New

- **Transitions between songs.** Choose Off, Crossfade or Automix in Settings; Mix playlists play their own transitions.
- **An AI DJ.** It picks and plays music through your own Claude Code, and asks before it removes or renames anything.
- **Upcoming songs load ahead.** The next tracks are fetched into the audio cache, so skipping and the next song start without waiting on the network.
- **Mirror and LED visualizers** in the player bar, next to Spectrum and Waveform.
- **The sidebar takes the page's cover colour.**
- **Choose what the Library sidebar shows:** its sections, a sort control, and By You.
- **Shelves scroll with arrows** instead of a scroll bar.
- **An opt-in profiler** and a native performance build, for finding slow frames.

## Fixed

- **Your playlists still list while Spotify's Web API turns requests away**, read from your account's playlist list instead.
- **The artwork disk cache stays under 512 MiB.**

## From crmne 0.13.1 to 0.13.3

A frame rate setting for the visualizers, F for full-screen lyrics, playback that reconnects by itself after a network drop, no more freezes while the disk is busy or stalls with a large MilkDrop presets folder, Google Cast speakers checked as genuine, and stronger sign-in, cover art downloads and MilkDrop preset loading. See crmne's [0.13.1](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.13.1), [0.13.2](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.13.2) and [0.13.3](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.13.3) notes.
